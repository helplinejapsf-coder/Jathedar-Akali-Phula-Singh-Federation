Jathedar Akali Phula Singh Federation is presented on this website as a non-profit organisation. Any donation or contribution is voluntary and should be made only through payment details officially confirmed by the Federation.

Unless required by applicable law or expressly stated in writing when a contribution is accepted, voluntary donations may not be refundable. If a donor believes a payment was mistaken, duplicated or unauthorised, they should contact the Federation promptly for review.

3. Event or activity payments

If a fee is collected for a specific event, programme or activity, applicable cancellation and refund conditions should be communicated before payment. Those written conditions apply subject to applicable law.

Email the payment date, amount, transaction reference and a brief explanation. Do not send passwords, PINs, CVV numbers or full card details.
